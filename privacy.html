This Privacy Policy explains how 2MinPay ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your data.

1. What Data We Collect

For Developers:

  • Email address
  • Stripe account information (connected via OAuth)
  • Application configuration (plans, pricing, etc.)
  • API keys and credentials

For End Users (your customers):

  • Email address (captured from Stripe checkout or during restore flow)
  • License status and subscription information
  • Temporary client ID (for cross-device sync)

We do NOT collect:

  • Credit card numbers — all payments handled by Stripe
  • Browser history or browsing behavior
  • Personal messages or content from your application

2. How We Collect Data

  • Developer data: Provided when you sign up or configure your application
  • End user email: Received from Stripe after successful payment OR entered during restore flow
  • Temporary client ID: Generated automatically by our SDK on first use
  • License status: Generated based on payment information from Stripe webhooks

3. How We Use Data

  • To verify license status when isPaid() is called
  • To enable cross-device sync via magic links
  • To process payments through Stripe
  • To send important service updates (rare, only critical)
  • To improve our service and fix bugs

We do not sell your data or end user data to third parties.

4. Data Sharing

We share data only when necessary:

  • Stripe: To process payments. We send end user email and payment amount. Stripe's Privacy Policy applies.
  • Email service: To send magic links for restore flow (we use [Your Email Service Provider]).
  • Legal requirements: If required by law or court order.

5. Data Retention

  • Developer data: Retained as long as your account is active. Deleted within 30 days after account deletion.
  • End user data: Retained for the duration of the license + reasonable period for billing disputes (up to 1 year after license expiration).
  • Email addresses: Stored until user requests deletion or license expires.

6. Your Rights (GDPR, CCPA)

If you are in the EU, UK, or California, you have the right to:

  • Access your data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Export your data
  • Object to processing

To exercise these rights, contact us at contact@2minpay.com. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data:

  • All API communication uses TLS 1.2+ encryption
  • Passwords and API keys are hashed
  • JWT tokens are short-lived and automatically refreshed
  • Access to production data is restricted to authorized personnel only

However, no system is 100% secure. Use at your own risk.

8. Children's Privacy

2MinPay is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided data to us, contact us to delete it.

9. Third-Party Links

Our service may contain links to Stripe or other third-party sites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Review their policies before using them.

10. International Data Transfers

We operate globally. Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries with different data protection laws. By using 2MinPay, you consent to such transfers.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Material changes will be announced via email or through your dashboard at least 14 days in advance. Continued use of 2MinPay after changes constitutes acceptance.

12. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions or to exercise your rights:

Email: contact@2minpay.com
